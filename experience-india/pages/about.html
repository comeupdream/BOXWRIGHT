What we do, in one breath.

We help people experience India through curated cultural journeys, and we help international patients navigate the non-clinical journey around healthcare in India: understanding the process, connecting with providers, and coordinating accommodation, transport and documentation.

What we are not.

  • A generic sightseeing-tour website or travel-agency catalogue
  • A hospital, clinic or doctor
  • A site that makes promises about medical outcomes, prices or rankings

Where we are today.

We are building in the open. Our first experience is in design, our interest list is open, and our medical facilitation pages will only ever carry claims we have verified. Original photography and film from India will replace our illustrations as we travel. If you want to follow the journey, we post every day on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
