How it works

Five steps. No surprises.

This is how a cultural experience with us unfolds, from the first message to the last farewell dinner.

  1. Inquiry

    You join the interest list for an experience, or tell us which festival you would like to join. Name, email, country, rough dates and group size. Two minutes. No deposit, no passport details, no payment.

  2. Consultation

    We reply personally. A short call or email exchange about your dates, who is traveling, your comfort with crowds and late nights, and what you hope the trip will feel like. We tell you honestly whether the experience is right for you.

  3. Reservation

    When itinerary, inclusions and pricing are confirmed, interest-list members are invited to reserve first. You receive full terms, a payment schedule and cancellation policy before you commit to anything.

  4. Preparation

    Visa guidance, flight timing, health and packing notes, and cultural briefings so you understand the festival before you land. A group introduction so you arrive among friends.

  5. Experience

    You arrive. Hosts meet you. From that moment, logistics are ours and the moment is yours.

What we ask of you.

  • Come with curiosity and respect. You are a guest at a religious festival as well as a traveler.
  • Be comfortable with a small group, shared meals and a daily plan.
  • Tell us early about mobility, dietary or health needs so we can plan around them.
  • Handle your own international flights and travel insurance. We will tell you what to book and when.
