How medical travel works

The patient journey, step by step.

Who does what at each stage. Amber steps are decisions that are yours. Teal steps are where we, or your providers, do the work.

Experience India or the providerYour decision

  1. 1. Inquiry

    You submit a short, private inquiry: the broad area of treatment you are considering, timing, whether you would travel with an attendant, and how to reach you. No medical records at this stage.

    You start it

  2. 2. Information and consent

    We reply with a plain explanation of the process, what a provider is likely to need, and how information is handled. If you want to proceed, you give informed consent for us to share your inquiry with providers.

    Experience India

  3. 3. Provider connection

    We help you connect with hospitals or providers appropriate to your inquiry, and explain how to send your records to them securely.

    Experience India

  4. 4. Provider evaluation

    The hospital's doctors review your case and tell you whether and how they can help. This is a clinical judgement, made by clinicians, not by us.

    Hospital / provider

  5. 5. Quotation

    The provider gives you an estimate of costs, likely duration of stay and what is included. Ask questions. We help you understand the answers.

    Hospital / provider

  6. 6. Your decision

    You decide whether to go ahead, with whom, and when. There is no pressure and no obligation to us at any point.

    Your decision

  7. 7. Visa and travel

    India's e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant visas are applied for online. We help with documentation support and the provider's invitation letter, flight timing and arrival planning.

    Experience India, where engaged

  8. 8. In India

    Airport pickup, accommodation near the hospital, transport to appointments, local SIM, and someone to call. The treatment itself is entirely between you and your providers.

    Experience India (non-clinical) and the provider (clinical)

  9. 9. Non-clinical logistics during your stay

    Extending accommodation, arranging for attendants, and day-to-day coordination while you recover.

    Experience India, where engaged

  10. 10. Follow-up coordination

    After you return, where contracted, we help coordinate follow-up communication between you and your provider.

    Experience India, where engaged
