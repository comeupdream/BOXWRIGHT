Why Ganesh Chaturthi

Why Ganesh Chaturthi

Ten days when a whole city celebrates together.

Ganesh Chaturthi honours Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. Families and neighbourhoods welcome clay idols into their homes and public pavilions, offer prayers, music and sweets, and after ten days carry them in enormous processions to the water.

It is public, welcoming and joyous by nature. Neighbourhoods compete on decoration and generosity, strangers are fed, and music never really stops. For a traveler, that openness is the door in.

What to expect: late nights, loud drums, big crowds on procession days, and a great deal of warmth. We plan quieter days around the peaks.

The experience

Festival, food, community, place.

Festival

Idol-making workshops before the festival begins. Home and neighbourhood pavilion visits. Evening aartis. A day inside the great immersion processions, with a safe vantage and hosts who know the crowd.

Food

Festival sweets made in a family kitchen. Regional thalis. Street food walks with someone who knows exactly which stall. A cooking session you will actually repeat at home.

Community

Time with the families and neighbourhood groups who host the celebration. Not a performance for tourists: their festival, and you are invited.

Place

Days of calm between the loud ones: coast, countryside or old town, chosen to balance the festival's intensity. Comfortable hotels and private transport throughout.
