What is Medical Value Travel?

Traveling abroad for healthcare, with the journey around it organised.

Medical Value Travel (MVT) is when a patient travels to another country for medical treatment. India is one of the countries international patients consider, and it has a government-recognised framework for it, including dedicated e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant visa categories.

Treatment itself is always delivered by hospitals and doctors. Around it sits everything else: understanding options, contacting providers, sending records, visas, flights, where to stay, how to get to appointments, and what happens when you go home. That "everything else" is where a facilitator helps.

The journey around the treatment

What we do

Facilitation and non-clinical coordination.

  • Explain the medical travel process to India in plain language
  • Help you connect with hospitals and providers appropriate to your inquiry
  • Help you understand what information and documents a provider may ask for
  • Where you engage us: coordinate accommodation, ground transport and documentation support such as visa paperwork
  • Support for attendants and family traveling with you
  • Coordinate follow-up communication after you return, where contracted

What we do not do

Anything a doctor should.

  • We do not diagnose, prescribe or give medical advice
  • We do not decide, or recommend, your treatment
  • We do not guarantee outcomes, results or timelines
  • We do not claim any hospital or treatment is "the best"
  • We do not quote savings percentages or cost promises
  • We do not handle medical records through ordinary web forms

The journey

From first inquiry to follow-up.

Every decision that matters stays with you and your providers. Our role is to make each step clear and the logistics manageable.

  1. Inquiry

    You tell us, in general terms, what you are considering.

  2. Information and consent

    We explain the process and what may be needed. You decide whether to proceed.

  3. Provider connection

    We help you connect with appropriate hospitals or providers.

  4. Evaluation and quotation

    The provider evaluates and quotes. Your decision.

  5. Travel and follow-up

    Visa, travel, stay and transport coordinated. Follow-up where contracted.

For patients

What you may be asked for.

Providers typically need a summary of your condition, recent reports or imaging, current medications and your treating doctor's notes. We will tell you when and how to share these safely, through a secure channel with your consent. Never through the inquiry form.

For attendants

Family and companions.

Most patients travel with someone. India offers an e-Medical Attendant visa for companions. We help with the paperwork, accommodation near the hospital, and getting around, so the person supporting you is supported too.

Trust

A transparent process, and provider-dependent decisions.

You will always know what we do, what we do not do, and who is making each decision. Costs, suitability and timelines come from the provider, in writing. Where we are paid for a service, we will tell you.

Specific claims about hospitals, prices, outcomes or accreditation appear on this site only after verification and review. If you do not see a claim here, it is because we have not verified it yet.
