Medical Value Travel

Are you a hospital or medical provider?

No. We are a facilitator. We do not diagnose, treat, prescribe or guarantee outcomes. All medical decisions are made by qualified providers and by you.

How does the medical-travel service work?

Inquiry, information and consent, provider connection, provider evaluation, quotation, your decision, visa and travel, non-clinical support in India, follow-up coordination. Every step is described on how medical travel works.

Which hospitals do you work with?

We connect patients with hospitals and providers appropriate to their inquiry. We do not publish rankings or "best hospital" claims, and we name partners only once relationships are verified.

How much will treatment cost? How much will I save?

Costs come from the provider, in writing, after evaluation. We do not publish savings percentages or price promises because they cannot be honest before a provider has seen your case.

Should I send my medical records now?

No. Please do not send records through the website or by ordinary email. When a provider needs them, we explain a secure way to share them, with your consent.

Can a family member travel with me?

Yes. India offers an e-Medical Attendant visa for companions. We help with the paperwork and logistics for attendants.
