Last updated: to be set at launch. This draft describes our intended practice and will be finalised with legal counsel before launch.

What we collect

When you use our forms we collect the details you enter: name, email, country, preferences and any message you choose to write. We also record where you came from (for example, a social-media link) to understand which content is useful. We do not collect passport numbers, payment details or medical records through this website.

How we use it

To reply to you, to send updates you have asked for, and to plan our experiences. Medical inquiries are used only to explain the process and, with your explicit consent, to connect you with a hospital or provider. We do not sell your information.

Medical information

Please do not send medical records through our forms or ordinary email. If a provider needs them, we will explain a secure process and ask for your consent first. Medical records shared with a provider are governed by that provider's own privacy practices.

Who sees it

Our team, and the service providers we use to run the website, host forms and send email. Hospitals or providers only see your information when you have asked us to make a connection.

Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages and videos bring people to the site. You can opt out with your browser's settings.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from updates at any time, and ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing {{ brand.email }}.
